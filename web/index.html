Your data, before you start.

Tidal Loom keeps a short motion window in memory and clears it when you stop. Camera images stay on your device. The SDK automatically sends derived measurements to Presage and Anthropic for AI analysis; that processing cannot currently be disabled. Optional telemetry is disabled here, but authentication and usage records remain. Presage retains analysis records while the developer account is active, then normally deletes them within 60 days of account closure; other retention periods apply. Stopping clears local data, not those server records. For deletion requests and full details, read the SmartSpectra SDK privacy policy.

If you choose Record artwork, the app temporarily keeps a video of the ribbons and source label in memory. It contains no camera images, audio or API key. Download saves it to your device; a new recording or closing the app discards the in-memory copy. Delete downloaded files on your device when you no longer want them. Nothing is uploaded by the recorder.
